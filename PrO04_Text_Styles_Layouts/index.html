Employee Handbook

Working Hours

Regular working hours run from nine to five. Employees are encouraged to plan leave requests at least three days in advance so that teams can arrange cover.

Good typography makes policies easy to read and hard to misunderstand.

Leave Rules

Casual leave is limited to twelve days per year and may be taken in half-day units. Sick leave beyond two days requires a medical certificate.

Earned leave accumulates monthly and can be carried forward up to thirty days. Approval is given by the reporting manager through the portal.
